Fun facts from the book

1937 · Singapore

Abu Bakar opened Kasab Kadai in Little India — the first halal butchery to import Australian meat, and the first to use a meat mincer.

1958 · Neravy

His clock tower's clock came from Germany. Descendants restored it in 2023 — it also appears for a few seconds in the Tamil film Lal Salaam.

1940s · Joo Chiat Rd

Sultan Maricar, half-Malay, ran a printing shop in Singapore before returning to India for a floral business in Karikal.

1935 · Japan

Amina's mother Alimah Ummal's own brother served as a Governor in Japan.

1950s · Singapore

Kuddus stayed a farmer in Sangamangalam his whole life — but sent all three sons to Singapore, where their descendants still run the family shop.

2025

Faherah's own parents are first cousins — the tangle she set out to untangle by writing this book.
